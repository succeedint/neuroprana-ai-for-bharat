The Science of Breath. The Wisdom of Balance.

Sushumna is a breath-based wellness app that offers a brief check-in followed by a personalized breathing practice tailored to how you're feeling in the moment.

About Sushumna

Sushumna combines timeless yogic breathing practices with modern technology to help people pause, breathe, and restore balance in everyday life.

Getting started

From the home screen, tap Begin to answer a few short questions about your current stress, mood, and mental clarity. Based on your responses, Sushumna recommends a personalized breathing practice with step-by-step guidance you can follow at your own pace.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. Sushumna doesn't require sign-in, and it doesn't collect your name or email address.

Why does the app ask about my stress, mood, and clarity every time?
Your mental and emotional state can change throughout the day. Sushumna asks a few brief questions each session so it can recommend the most appropriate breathing practice for how you're feeling in the moment.

What if I don't have an internet connection?
Sushumna will still generate a recommendation on your device if it can't reach our servers, so you're never stuck without a practice to follow.

Is Sushumna a substitute for medical or mental health care?
No. Sushumna is intended to support general wellness and mindfulness. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease and should not replace professional medical or psychological care. If you feel dizzy, uncomfortable, or unwell during a practice, stop and return to natural breathing. If you're experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, please contact a licensed professional or emergency services in your area.

Supported devices

Sushumna is designed for iPhone running a supported version of iOS. For the best experience, keep your device updated and connected to the internet.
